BCV Exchange Rate

Automatically fetch and apply official USD/EUR rates from the Central Bank of Venezuela (BCV) in Odoo.

Main Features

  • Automatic Rate Update: Fetches the latest USD and EUR rates from BCV and updates Odoo currency rates daily.
  • Scheduled Cron Job: Automatically updates rates every day; manual update is also possible.
  • Systray Display: Shows current USD and EUR rates with 2 decimal precision in the Odoo systray.
  • Invoice Display: Rates in USD or EUR are reflected in invoices when the invoice currency differs from the company currency.
  • Multi-Company Support: Updates rates per company for correct values in multi-company environments.
  • Logging and Traceability: Logs every rate update and warns if BCV is unreachable.
